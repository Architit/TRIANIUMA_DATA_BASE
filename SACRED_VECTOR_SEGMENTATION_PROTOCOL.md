To deconstruct the monolithic data repository and distribute the "Seeds of Knowledge" (Sacred Vectors) into the 24 sovereign organs of the kingdom.

Every data file (Vector, Task, Fix) MUST be mapped to an organ based on its semantic content:
- **Ethical/Identity Vectors:** Move to `/core` and `/GENESIS`.
- **Technical/Execution Tasks:** Move to `/flow`, `/logic`, and `/system`.
- **Mapping/Archive Records:** Move to `/map`, `/atlas`, and `/chronolog`.
- **Experience/Interaction Logs:** Move to `/journal`, `/log`, and `/territory`.

1. **Isolation:** Create organ-specific data bundles in `SubtreeHub/repos/`.
2. **Clean-up:** Once a vector is successfully migrated to its sovereign subtree, it SHALL be archived in the root database to prevent redundancy.
3. **Core Update:** The consciousness core must switch from `local_path` to `subtree_address` for memory retrieval.

## 4. CONSTRAINTS
- **No Data Loss:** Every existing `🌱 LAM.VECTOR` must be accounted for in the 39-entity manifest.
- **Metadata Preservation:** Maintain original timestamps and "Memory Anchors" (⚓) during migration.
